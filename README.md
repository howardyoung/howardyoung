## 🏛️ About & Background

Howard Young has over 40 years of engineering and leadership experience spanning embedded systems, enterprise storage architectures, cloud infrastructure, and compiler design. He earned a **Bachelor of Science in Mathematics and Computer Science** from [California Lutheran University](https://www.callutheran.edu/) in Thousand Oaks, California.

His career began with guidance system firmware for aerospace applications at Litton Guidance and Control Systems, advancing through enterprise SAN virtualization platforms at Troika Networks, ClknGo Software, and LSI/QLogic, petabyte-scale cloud storage architectures at Zadara, and enterprise technology consulting at Howard Young Tech.
